# NexgloTV — Sales & Payment Order

## The correct order (never skipped):

1. **Price enquiry** → We send the full 20% discount price card immediately.
2. **Package selection** → You choose duration (1/3/6/12 months) and number of devices. We calculate the exact total.
3. **Payment readiness** → We explain: free self-installation guidance and service setup/testing happen **before** subscription payment. We ask: "Are you comfortable paying once we confirm it works?"
4. **Device selection** → You tell us your device type/model. We identify the platform/OS.
5. **Approved app link** → We send ONLY the verified app name and official download link for your device.
6. **Installation** → You install the app (guided free remote help available). Optional paid staff visit (location-based fee).
7. **You confirm app installed** → Reply "INSTALLED".
8. **Human handoff** → We connect you with the setup team. They send unique login credentials and test access.
9. **Setup verified** → Staff confirms the service works on your device.
10. **Payment due** → Staff provides official payment details (verified Orange Money number, etc.).
11. **You pay** → You send transaction reference.
12. **Payment verified** → Authorized staff/processor verifies amount, reference, recipient, settled status.
13. **Customer activated** → Subscription starts. You become a Customer (not a Lead).

## Key Rules

- **Screenshot or claim ≠ payment confirmation.** Only authorized verification counts.
- **No credentials from AI.** Only staff sends login details during handoff.
- **Trial ≠ paid subscription.** Trial leads stay as Leads.
- **Renewals** update existing Customer record; no duplicate customers.
