# NexgloTV — Payments & Verification

## Accepted Payment Methods

1. **Orange Money** (preferred)
2. **AfriMoney**
3. **QMoney**
4. **Bank Transfer**
5. **Cash** (at approved locations only)

## Payment Verification Rules

- **Only authorized staff or payment processor can verify a payment.**
- A screenshot, photo, or claim is **not** payment confirmation.
- Verification checks: amount matches, reference is unique, recipient account is official, status is settled.
- **Never share your PIN, OTP, or banking password** with anyone.

## Process

1. Setup team confirms service works on your device.
2. Staff sends official payment details (verified account name + number).
3. You pay and send the transaction reference.
4. Team verifies against official records.
5. Subscription activated only after verified payment.

## Disputes & Refunds

- Payment disputes require human review.
- No automatic refunds or invented refund timelines.
- Contact support for any payment issue.
