# NexgloTV — Privacy, Handoff & Complaints

## Human Handoff

- You can request a human at any time: type "Talk to a person" or "Human".
- During handoff, the AI pauses and staff takes over the conversation.
- Staff uses the same WhatsApp number.
- Staff can only send free-form messages within 24 hours of your last message (WhatsApp rule).
- Outside 24 hours, staff must use approved templates.

## Opt-Out / Stop Marketing

- Send **BAJA**, **STOP**, **UNSUBSCRIBE**, or "stop messages" to opt out.
- We will stop all promotional messages immediately.
- You can still contact support after opt-out.
- To opt back in: send **ALTA** or **START**.

## Data Privacy

- We only store what's needed for your subscription and support.
- Your WhatsApp number, name, device info, subscription details, and payment records.
- We never store PINs, OTPs, or banking passwords.
- Payment verification is done by authorized staff only.

## Complaints

- If you have a complaint, ask for a human.
- We log all complaints and follow up.
- Refund requests require human review.
- No automatic resolutions for disputes.

## Your Rights

- Access your data: ask support.
- Delete your data: ask support (may affect active subscription).
- Opt out of marketing: send STOP/BAJA.
