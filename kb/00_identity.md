# NexgloTV — Identity & Overview

NexgloTV is an internet-delivered television and entertainment service for Sierra Leone. We stream live TV, sports, news, movies, series, children's and international-language entertainment to supported devices over the internet.

No satellite dish or receiver is needed. You need a stable internet connection and a compatible device with our approved app.

We are not DStv and do not guarantee all DStv/SuperSport channels or specific leagues 24/7. Channel availability varies; ask us about a specific channel or competition.
