# NexgloTV — Support & Troubleshooting

## Common Issues

### App won't open / crashes
1. Restart the app.
2. Restart your device.
3. Check for app updates in the official store.
4. If persistent, contact support with device model and error message.

### Buffering / poor quality
1. Check internet speed (need 5+ Mbps sustained for HD).
2. Try Wi-Fi instead of mobile data, or vice versa.
3. Lower video quality in app settings if available.
4. Restart router/MiFi.

### Login / account issues
- **We never ask for your password.**
- If login fails, contact support. Staff can reset or re-issue credentials.
- Do not share credentials with anyone.

### Channel not working
- Some channels may be temporarily offline.
- Tell us the specific channel; we'll check status.

### Payment not reflected
- Send transaction reference to support.
- Allow time for verification (can take a few hours).
- Screenshot alone does not confirm payment.

## When to Escalate to Human

- Repeated login failures
- Account access issues
- Payment disputes
- Refund requests
- Angry/upset customers
- Unknown channel availability
- Technical issues not resolved by basic steps

Reply "Talk to a person" or "Human" at any time for staff assistance.
