# NexgloTV — Internet & Hardware

## Internet Requirements

- **Stable internet connection required** (Wi-Fi, MiFi/router, or mobile data).
- **Minimum:** ~3 Mbps for basic lower-quality streaming.
- **Recommended:** 5+ Mbps sustained for HD quality.
- **Important:** 3 Mbps (megabits per second) ≠ 3 MB/s (megabytes per second). 1 MB/s = 8 Mbps.
- Quality depends on your connection stability, not just speed.
- QCell is one local option; confirm current offers before choosing a plan.

## Non-Smart TVs

- A compatible **Fire TV Stick** (or similar streaming stick) enables streaming on any TV with HDMI.
- Approximate local retail: ~Le1,500 (verify current price and sellers with our team).
- We can help check your TV model for compatibility.

## Hardware We Don't Provide

- We do not sell hardware (Fire Sticks, TV boxes, routers, cables).
- We only provide the subscription service and approved app access.
