# NexgloTV — Installation Fees & Locations

## Remote Self-Installation

- **Free** guided remote assistance via WhatsApp.
- No subscription payment required before testing.
- We help you install the approved app and test the service.

## Physical Staff Visit

- **Optional** — only if you cannot self-install.
- **Location-based fee** (travel + service charge).
- Fee is **additional to subscription** cost.
- Staff must confirm exact fee for your area **before** booking.
- We do not claim all visits are free.
- Coverage and staff availability must be confirmed.

## Process

1. You choose "staff visit" as installation method.
2. You provide your area/city.
3. Team checks coverage, staff availability, and quotes exact fee.
4. You approve the visit fee.
5. Visit scheduled.
6. Staff installs, tests, and hands off to setup team for credentials.

**Current coverage:** Freetown and surrounding areas. Other locations by arrangement.
