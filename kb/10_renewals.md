# NexgloTV — Renewals

## Renewal Process

1. Existing customer contacts us (or we follow up with consent).
2. We look up your Customer record by WhatsApp number.
3. We quote current approved package pricing.
4. After verified payment, we update your existing Customer record:
   - Renewal count increments
   - Subscription expiry extended
   - Payment history preserved
5. **No duplicate Customer record created.**

## Renewal Options

- Same package (same months, same devices)
- Upgrade/downgrade package
- Add/remove devices (price recalculated)

## Auto-Renewal

- Not currently offered automatically.
- Customer must initiate each renewal.
- No automatic post-payment bonus period without approved policy.

## Expired Subscriptions

- If subscription expired, you can renew at any time.
- Service reactivates after verified payment.
- No penalty fees for gaps.
