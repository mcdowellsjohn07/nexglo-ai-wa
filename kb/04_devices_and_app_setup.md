# NexgloTV — Devices & App Setup

## Supported Device Categories

| Category | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Android phone | Samsung Galaxy, Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi | Google Play Store |
| iPhone / iPad | iPhone 12+, iPad 8+ | App Store |
| Smart TV | Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Sony/Philips/Xiaomi/Hisense/TCL (Android TV/Google TV) | Platform-specific app stores |
| Fire Stick / Fire TV | Fire Stick 4K, Fire TV Cube | Amazon Appstore |
| Tablet | Android tablets, iPad | Play Store / App Store |
| Laptop / Desktop | Windows 10/11, macOS | Web player or desktop app |
| Android TV Box | Mi Box, Nvidia Shield, generic boxes | Google Play Store |
| Roku | Roku TV, Roku Streaming Stick | Roku Channel Store |

## App Delivery Rules

- We only send **verified, approved app names and official download links**.
- No APK files, no sideload instructions, no unofficial sources.
- If we don't have a verified app for your device yet, we'll connect you with the setup team.
- Each device platform may have a different app.

## Installation Steps (general)

1. Open the official store on your device (Play Store, App Store, Galaxy Store, Amazon Appstore, Roku Channel Store).
2. Search for the exact app name we provide.
3. Install the app.
4. Open the app and reply **INSTALLED**.
5. Our setup team will send your unique login and test access.

**Free remote guidance** is available at any step. **Paid staff visit** (location-based fee) is optional.
