# NexgloTV — Free Trial Policy

## Pre-payment free trials are available

- Free trials can be requested **before any payment**.
- Trial duration, eligibility and activation require **staff approval**.
- Trials are **not automatically advertised** at first contact.
- If you ask for a trial, we'll check with our team for the current available duration.
- A trial account is temporary and controlled by staff.
- Trial users remain in **Leads** (not Customers) until verified payment.
- Trial does not automatically convert to paid; no auto-billing.
- The previous "7 extra days after payment" policy is **not currently active** unless re-approved.

**To request a trial:** Tell us which device you'd use and we'll have the team confirm availability.
