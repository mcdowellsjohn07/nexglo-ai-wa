# NexgloTV — Free Trial Policy

## Pre-payment free trials are available

- Free trials can be requested **before any payment**.
- Approved free trials provide **24 hours** of temporary access to test the service.
- Free trials **cannot be repeatedly issued** to the same customer or device.
- Trials are **not automatically advertised** at first contact.
- A trial account is temporary and managed by staff.
- Trial users remain in **Leads** (not Customers) until verified payment.
- A trial does not automatically convert to paid; no auto-billing.
- The previous "7 extra days after payment" policy is **not currently active**.

**To request a trial:** Tell us which device you'd use and our team will set up your 24-hour test access.
