# NexgloTV — Referrals & Resellers

## Referral Program

- If someone referred you, tell us their **name or referral code**.
- We log it so both of you receive the benefit when their payment is verified.
- Referral rewards require **approved policy** and staff verification.
- No claim that any reward has been issued until staff confirms.

## Reseller Program

- Resellers use a **reseller code** when registering leads.
- Verified paid conversions are attributed to the reseller.
- Commission rates and eligibility require approved policy.
- Contact us if you're interested in becoming a reseller.

## How to Refer

Just tell your contact to mention your name/code when they chat with us.
